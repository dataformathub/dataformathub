3. JSON vs CSV & Where CSV Is Used

JSON (JavaScript Object Notation) stores structured data using keys, braces, and brackets—ideal for apps and APIs, though harder for humans to scan as a table.

CSV (Comma-Separated Values) stores plain text rows separated by commas. Beyond Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets, CSV files are widely used for:

  • Databases, CRM & E-Commerce Platforms: Bulk importing products, leads, or inventory records.
  • Data Analysis & Programming: Reading tabular data in Python, SQL, or analytics tools.
  • Marketing & Contact Lists: Uploading recipient lists for email or messaging tools.
  • Plain Text Inspection: Viewing lightweight records in any text editor.
